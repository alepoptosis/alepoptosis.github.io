a.v. writes about a.v.

A.V. Avallone (he/they) is an Italian trans man living in Scotland. He's a data analyst and a director of Typewronger CIC — a non profit bookshop and creative space. He is passionate about post-apocalyptic fiction, indie videogames, and other people. He started a community network for transmascs in Edinburgh. Between number crunching and bookselling he writes zines about indecision and creative joy. Find him @victorsbookshelf on Instagram for book reviews and here for everything else.

Disclaimer: I am not a web developer. I am simply someone who cannot stand the current state of most free blogging platforms out there. I made this after watching a youtube video by onionboots about how the old internet isn't dead, we just aren't on it. I have done absolutely zero web development before this, but I like the idea of hand-crafting a space for myself and my words on the internet, so I learned the basics and here I am. All constructive feedback is welcome.
